THE CHECK
DeepSeek and GPT relabel 1,200 videos independently
κ 0.78
our model against them, on group labels
substantial agreement, well above chance
It misses 21% of group labels and flags 1% of other videos by mistake; every share we report is corrected for both.
A GENDER GROUP LABEL · ALL FOUR MUST HOLD
① a whole gender, not one person   ② a negative trait or blame
③ all or most of them   ④ in the video's own voice
Quoting it in order to rebut it does not count.
We count labels against either gender, and take no side on marriage.

14 — Takeaway
Keep the topic, widen the view
WHAT A USER CAN DO · THEIR OWN LAYER
Change what you finish: scroll past, delete, look for the other side
Put a filter in front of the feed
Keep doing it
WHAT ONLY THE PLATFORM CAN DO
Measure the view inside each topic
Publish the reference that makes skew visible
Make "not interested" mean not interested
Decide how much ranking amplifies what provokes
