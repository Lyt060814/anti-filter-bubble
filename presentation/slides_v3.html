14 — Escape · RQ4
Skew rebounds after the intervention ends

15 — Takeaway
Keep the topic, widen the view
WHAT A USER CAN DO · THEIR OWN LAYER
Change what you finish: scroll past, delete, look for the other side
Put a filter in front of the feed
Keep doing it
WHAT ONLY THE PLATFORM CAN DO
Measure the view inside each topic
Publish the reference that makes skew visible
Make "not interested" mean not interested
Decide how much ranking amplifies what provokes
