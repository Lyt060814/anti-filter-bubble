## Addendum (2026-09-28): claims checked against full texts

The two claims marked NOT FOUND above (abstract-only check) were confirmed against the full-text PDFs read in the deep dive (`docs/research/deep_dive/B_tsinghua_cocoon_lineage.md`):
- Li et al. (2022): the paper names Kuaishou explicitly (Kuaishou Inc. co-authors; 569,975 new users registered 2021-01-01, tracked for one year) and compares diversity of *exposed* vs *watched* videos.
- Piao et al. (2023): the model explains cocoons by similarity-based matching and positive/negative feedback; user self-exploration (σ) and stronger negative feedback (|γ₋|) push the system back toward diversity.
