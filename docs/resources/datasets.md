# Resource inventory 2: public datasets for an information-cocoon (信息茧房) / filter-bubble project

Prepared 2026-09-27 for the HKU COMP1110 group project.

**How this was checked.** Everything comes from web-search results or GitHub README pages opened on 2026-09-27. Zenodo, Kaggle, HuggingFace, kuairec.com, tenrec0.github.io and communitynotes.x.com could not be opened because the network blocked them. For those, the details come from search snippets only. Any detail I could not confirm on a primary page is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Nothing was downloaded. The web-search budget ran out partway through, so a few candidates were never checked. They are listed in §7 as "unchecked leads".

**Column key.** Imp = has impressions/exposures (what was shown, not only what was clicked). Neg = has explicit negative feedback (dislike / hate / not interested / skip). TS = timestamps. Cat = category, topic or tag labels. Rand = has a random-exposure subset. Rating = usefulness for our project (High / Med / Low).

## 7. Gaps, caveats and unchecked leads

**Gaps (no public data found):**
- No public **Xiaohongshu, Douyin or WeChat Channels feed-impression logs with "not interested" (不感兴趣) events**. Qilin (Xiaohongshu) is search-centred and its download location was not verified. NineRec has Bilibili/Douyin interactions but no impressions or negative feedback.
- No dataset logs use of the **anti-cocoon / "turn off personalised recommendation" switch** that China's 2022 algorithm-recommendation regulation requires. There is no public before/after data. Mozilla (YouTube) and Liu, Wu & Resnick (ICWSM 2024) are the only quantitative evidence on whether controls work, and neither released raw data that I could find.
- **KuaiRand `is_hate`** is the only large-scale *explicit* negative feedback field paired with random exposure. Yambda has dislikes but no category labels (UNVERIFIED) and no randomisation.
- Audit datasets are **small, English- or Western-centred, and short-lived**. Mosnar et al. 2025 show that TikTok audits reproduce poorly. There is almost nothing auditing Douyin, Kuaishou or Xiaohongshu.
- **Survey microdata for China and HK** on algorithm awareness is not public. CNNIC gives only aggregates. Reuters DNR gives chart CSVs (full tables on request). HKPORI was not checked.
- Ratings datasets (MovieLens, Netflix, Amazon, Goodreads, Yelp) contain **no exposures**. Any narrowing seen there mixes user choice with the algorithm. Say this explicitly in the report.
- The literature is split on whether filter bubbles exist (Hosseinmardi 2024, Liu et al. 2025 and the Bilibili "heavy users" study find weak or no effects). The project should frame its question as testable, not assumed.

**Access caveats:** Tenrec (application terms UNVERIFIED), EB-NeRD (registration UNVERIFIED), Yahoo R3 (Webscope application), Pew (free login), WVS (form), MicroLens full (email). Spotify MPD is no longer on AIcrowd. LFM-2b availability is uncertain. Pushshift's public API is gone (use Academic Torrents or Arctic Shift). Most Chinese-platform sets are non-commercial or research-only. Cite the papers.

**Unchecked leads (the search budget ran out; not verified, so do not cite without checking):** Plista news dataset, Yahoo! R6 (Front Page Today Module with a random policy), Google Trends and Baidu Index (search-interest time series), Media Bias/Fact Check and NewsGuard as standalone lists (both are covered inside the Lin et al. aggregate), HKPORI surveys, KuaiComt / other Kuaishou comment datasets, Douyin-specific academic datasets, the OSF data link for Haroon et al. 2023, data-availability statements for Ibrahim et al. (Nature 2026) and Liu et al. (PNAS 2025).
