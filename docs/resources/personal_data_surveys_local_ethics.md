# Resource Inventory: Self-collected data, instruments, local context and ethics
COMP1110 group project, information cocoons / filter bubbles (信息茧房). Compiled 2026-09-27.

**How to read this.** Every item has a URL that was actually seen in search results or fetched pages. Anything not confirmed is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Most direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy, so many details come from search-result snippets. Check the key facts yourself before quoting them in the report. Usefulness: H/M/L = High/Medium/Low for a 5-person, 6-week first-year project.

### 2c. Feed logging (what the algorithm *offers*, not just what you watched)
- **No ready-made open-source extension for logging the Xiaohongshu web explore page or the YouTube homepage was found** (GitHub search returned nothing). You would have to write a small userscript or Chrome extension that saves note titles/IDs from the page you are viewing. Estimated effort: Med–High.
- **Scrapers (avoid for this project):** https://github.com/NanmiCoder/MediaCrawler (~66k stars; Playwright plus a logged-in browser; supports Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Kuaishou, Bilibili, Weibo, Tieba, Zhihu; "for learning purposes only", non-commercial). **This conflicts with the Xiaohongshu/X ToS, and there is a criminal precedent (see §5).** Do not use it to crawl other users' content.
- **Screen recording or screenshots plus OCR (recommended, low-tech):**
  - **PaddleOCR**: https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR (~90k stars, strong Chinese OCR). Extract note/video titles from phone screenshots of the first N feed items.
  - **Stanford Screenomics**: https://github.com/StanfordScreenomics/Platform (Android app framework that captures screenshots every few seconds for research. Heavy and privacy-intensive; use as a reference only).
  - Workflow: each participant takes a screenshot of the first 20 items of the Xiaohongshu 发现 / Bilibili 首页 / Douyin 推荐 feed at a fixed time each day, OCRs them, labels topics with an LLM, then computes per-day topic entropy.
- **Zero-shot topic classification of Chinese titles (LLM):** example comparisons of BERT fine-tuning vs LLM zero-shot on Chinese news: https://github.com/haoyuliu-ntu/chinese-text-classification , https://github.com/sun-zhengda/bert-vs-llm-text-classification . The PNAS paper by Gilardi et al. 2023 on LLM annotation, https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2305016120 , could not be fetched here: **UNVERIFIED**. Always validate on a hand-labelled sample of about 100 titles and report agreement (e.g., Cohen's κ).
- **Topic taxonomies:**
  - **IAB Tech Lab Content Taxonomy** (v1.0 to **3.1**, TSV): https://github.com/InteractiveAdvertisingBureau/Taxonomies/tree/main/Content%20Taxonomies . Use Tier 1 (about 30 top-level categories, count UNVERIFIED) as a standard label set, mapped to Chinese.
  - Bilibili 分区 (zone) names come with history records (field name, e.g. `tag_name`, UNVERIFIED), giving free platform-native categories.
  - Xiaohongshu explore-page channel tabs (推荐/穿搭/美食/彩妆/影视/职场/情感/家居/游戏/旅行/健身…): **list UNVERIFIED**, so record it from the app yourselves.

## 5. Ethics and rules

### 5a. HKU human-research ethics
- **Rule:** staff, research postgraduates, taught postgraduates **and undergraduates who are the PI of a research project involving the consent of human participants** must get ethics clearance. For undergraduates, **Heads of Department (or Deans of unitary Faculties) or their delegates vet the applications.** Applications are made with the Tutor/Supervisor as principal applicant via the department's form or HREC's (search-result summary).
- **Exemption:** only anonymous surveys **for improving teaching and learning (not research)** that are exclusively for internal University use. A course project survey of other students' platform use is therefore **not clearly exempt**.
- Processing: HREAS applications normally take ≤3 weeks. Approval is needed **before** any data collection.
- URLs: https://www.rss.hku.hk/integrity/ethics-compliance/hrec ; guidelines PDF https://www.rss.hku.hk/HREC/guidelines.pdf ; FAQ http://www.rss.hku.hk/HREC/faq.pdf ; HREAS http://www.rss.hku.hk/integrity/ethics-compliance/hreas ; form https://www.rss.hku.hk/HREC/ethical-approval-application-form.docx ; example departmental UG form (Psychology) https://psychology.hku.hk/ethics-application-psyc4007/
- **Whether COMP1110 has a blanket course-level clearance or treats class projects as "teaching exercises" is UNVERIFIED.** **Action for week 1:** email the course coordinator. With 6 weeks, (a) collect data only from the 5 team members (self-study / autoethnography), or (b) use a fully anonymous, minimal-risk survey only once the instructor confirms it's allowed.

### 5c. Sock puppets / research accounts
- **Sandvig v. Barr, 451 F.Supp.3d 73 (D.D.C. 2020):** a US federal court held that **violating a website's ToS alone does not make access "unauthorized" under the US CFAA** (researchers planned fictitious tester accounts to audit discrimination by hiring-site algorithms). URLs: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2020/04/federal-judge-rules-it-not-crime-violate-websites-terms-service ; https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2020/04/another-court-significantly-limits-the-scope-of-criminal-cfaa-sandvig-v-barr.htm ; https://www.aclu.org/cases/sandvig-v-barr-challenge-cfaa-prohibition-uncovering-racial-discrimination-online ; https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/sandvig-v-barr-civil-891252358
- **Caveat:** this is a US criminal-law ruling. It **does not apply in HK or mainland China** and does not protect against contract claims or account bans. The Shenzhen University study (§4) *did* use "账号模拟测评" (account simulation), so a precedent exists in Chinese academia. For students, prefer **each person using ONE fresh account registered in their own name** (not batch-created), browsed manually, with no posting or interaction with real users. **Whether Xiaohongshu allows a second personal account per phone number is UNVERIFIED.**

### 5d. Topic and data sensitivity (recommendation, not a sourced rule)
- Under PIPL and the PDPO, a donated history is personal data about the donor and can reveal sensitive traits (health, sexuality, politics). Process it locally, keep only title + category + timestamp, strip usernames and comments, and delete raw exports after the project.
- In the HK context, avoid measuring political-camp polarization (thermometers toward political groups). Use lifestyle, entertainment or consumer "camps" instead.
- Don't collect other users' content (comments, profiles) beyond the titles shown in your own feed.
