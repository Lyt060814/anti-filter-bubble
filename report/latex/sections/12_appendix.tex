\section{Proofs of the Propositions}
\label{app:proofs}

Throughout, $\pi(i)\propto q_i\exp\langle x,v_i\rangle$ as in \cref{eq:softmax}, with $x$ the state of one user, and $\mathbb{E}_G$ denotes the exposure-weighted mean over a set $G$ of videos.

\paragraph{\Cref{prop:feedback}.} After the update $x\mapsto x+\eta v_j$, each video's weight is multiplied by $e^{\eta s_j(i)}$, so $\pi'(i)=\pi(i)e^{\eta s_j(i)}/Z$ with $Z=\sum_i\pi(i)e^{\eta s_j(i)}$. For any set $G$,
\[
  \log\pi'(G)-\log\pi(G)=\log\mathbb{E}_G\bigl[e^{\eta s_j}\bigr]-\log Z=\eta\,\bar s_j(G)+\tfrac{\eta^2}{2}\operatorname{Var}_G(s_j)-\log Z+O(\eta^3).
\]
Since $V=\log\pi(\A)-\log\pi(\B)-\logit\pbar$ and $\pbar$ does not depend on $x$, the normalising term cancels and $\Delta V=\eta\,[\bar s_j(\A)-\bar s_j(\B)]+O(\eta^2)$. The same argument applied to $\logit m=\log\pi(\I_k)-\log\pi(\I\setminus\I_k)$ gives $\Delta\logit m=\eta\,t_j+O(\eta^2)$. \qed

\paragraph{\Cref{prop:topic}.} If every video in $\I_k$ has its exposure multiplied by $\lambda>0$ before renormalisation, then $\pi'(\A)/\pi'(\B)=\lambda\pi(\A)/\lambda\pi(\B)$, so $p$ and therefore $V$ are unchanged. For the second claim, suppose no category separates $\A$ from $\B$, so that every category containing videos of $\A$ also contains videos of $\B$. Then exposure can be moved between $\A$ and $\B$ inside each category without changing any category's total, which changes $p$ and $V$ but no category share; a statistic of category shares takes the same value before and after. \qed

\paragraph{\Cref{prop:filter}.} After filtering, the exposure that reaches the screen is $\pi(\A)(1-\mathrm{TPR})$ for the narrative and $\pi(\B)(1-\mathrm{FPR})$ for the retained set. Taking the log of their ratio gives $\logit p'=\logit p+\log\frac{1-\mathrm{TPR}}{1-\mathrm{FPR}}$. Their sum is $\pi(\I_k)\,[p(1-\mathrm{TPR})+(1-p)(1-\mathrm{FPR})]$, so the number of issue videos shown falls by the bracketed factor. (If hidden slots are refilled with other videos, the topic share falls slightly further, as in \cref{tab:escape}.) \qed

\paragraph{\Cref{prop:reach}.} $\Lambda=\logit\pbar-\logit\psup$ involves only the catalogue and the population average, so no change to one user's $x$ alters it. At $x=x^0$ the feed is the non-personalised one, whose within-issue share is $\pbar$ by the assumption of \cref{sec:layers}; hence $V=0$ and, by \cref{eq:decomp}, the total skew equals $\Lambda$. The same identity shows that the total is below $\Lambda$ exactly when $V<0$. If the state relaxes towards $x^0$ in the absence of signals, a state with $V<0$ is not stationary without them. \qed

\paragraph{\Cref{prop:detect}.} With $n$ videos in each sample the observed shares $\hat q_u,\hat q_0$ are approximately normal with variances $q_u(1-q_u)/n$ and $q_0(1-q_0)/n$. A one-sided level-$a$ test of $q_u>q_0$ has power $1-\gamma$ when $q_u-q_0=(z_a+z_\gamma)\sqrt{[q_u(1-q_u)+q_0(1-q_0)]/n}$. Solving for $n$ and substituting $q_u-q_0=(\alpha-\beta)(p_u-p_0)$ gives \cref{eq:detect}. \qed

\section{Simulation Model and Parameters}
\label{app:sim}

The simulator tracks four groups of videos $g\in\{\A,\ \B\text{ (ordinary)},\ \text{rebuttal},\ \text{other}\}$. The recommender keeps a profile $h_g$, an exponentially weighted count of the signals the user gave to each group, and forms two centred features from the profile shares $r_g=h_g/\sum_{g'}|h_{g'}|$:
\[
  f_T=r_{\text{issue}}-m_0,\qquad f_A=r_{\A}-r_{\text{rebuttal}}-\kappa\,r_{\text{issue}},
\]
where $r_{\text{issue}}$ sums the three issue groups and $\kappa$ is chosen so that a user who finishes issue videos in platform proportions has $f_A=0$. The next video is drawn with log-odds
\[
  \log\pi(g)=\log\pi_0(g)+\Delta_T\,\mathbf{1}[g\text{ on issue}]+\Delta_A\,a_g+\text{const},\qquad
  \Delta_\bullet=S_\bullet\tanh\!\bigl(G_\bullet f_\bullet/S_\bullet\bigr),
\]
with $a_g=+1$ for $\A$, $-1$ for rebuttals and $0$ otherwise, and $\pi_0$ the platform shares. This is \cref{eq:softmax} with a two-dimensional state and a saturating read-out. \Cref{tab:simparams} lists the parameters. The recommender's parameters are fixed by the logs and the probe; the user's are scenario choices.
